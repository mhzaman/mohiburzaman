03 — Research

PUBLICATION & RESEARCH

01
ICCIT '24 · Paper ID 252 · Accepted 20 Dec 2024
A Data-Driven Insight to Enhancing Stress Management Through Chatbot Interaction Among Undergraduate Students

Developed an intelligent assistive system that classifies stress levels among undergraduate students to support mental health management through chatbot interaction. Built a custom survey-based dataset using HCI techniques. Applied ML to train and compare Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, SVM, Random Forest, AdaBoost, Gradient Boosting and Naive Bayes. The best-performing Decision Tree model was integrated into the chatbot to predict stress/depression levels and deliver personalized management strategies.

DOI: 10.1109/ICCIT64611.2024.11022394 · 27th International Conference on Computer & Information Technology (ICCIT)

🔤 Banglish Text Error Correction

Implemented four ML/NLP techniques — SVM, Multinomial Naive Bayes, Random Forest, and Decision Tree — on a self-created Banglish dataset, evaluated across multiple metrics to identify the most suitable model for error detection and correction.

💻 Laptop Price Regression Analysis

Compared Linear Regression, KNN, Decision Tree, and Random Forest models for laptop price prediction — using four evaluation metrics to surface the most accurate model and generate insights for pricing and purchasing decisions.
